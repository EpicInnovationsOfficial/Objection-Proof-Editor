# Objection-Proof-Editor

Objection-Proof Editor — Sales Language Corrector
Built by Epic Innovations, Inc., shared for anyone who wants their sales writing and call language to stop triggering resistance before it starts.

What this is
A Claude skill that reviews the specific words and phrases used in sales emails, scripts, proposals, or call transcripts, and rewrites them to remove fear-, pressure-, obligation-, and cliché-triggering language — replacing it with calm, confident, collaborative phrasing. It works at the word/phrase level only: it doesn't restructure your pitch, change your offer, or grade your delivery — just the language most likely to put a prospect's guard up before they've even considered what you're saying.

Setup (2 minutes)

1. Copy this whole `objection-proof-editor` folder wherever your Claude surface loads skills from.
2. Have your sales email, script, or call transcript ready — paste it in or attach it.
3. Ask Claude to run the Objection-Proof Editor on it. You'll get back a rewritten version in the same format as your original (not a table), plus a short plain-language note on the handful of biggest changes and why.

Why word choice matters
Certain words and phrases reliably trigger a defensive reaction in a listener or reader — not because of what they mean, but because of the fear, pressure, or "salesy" cliché they carry. Swapping "contract" for "agreement," or "just following up" for language anchored to the actual conversation, doesn't change your offer — it changes whether the reader's guard goes up before they even get to it.

Modularity note
This skill is built to be called by broader sales-coaching skills — for example, Epic Innovations' own Sales Pitch Grader calls this skill for its word/phrase-level pass — while remaining fully usable on its own for a standalone language review.

Privacy
Whatever you submit for review is your own sales material. Nothing here is intended to train or improve any third-party model, and you shouldn't need to share your content outside your own Claude conversations.
